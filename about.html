About me

Hi, I’m Sang — a business analyst and product designer based in Da Nang, Vietnam, with an IT background and 7+ years in fintech and complex platforms.

Aligning business, design and engineering around one clear product direction — turning complexity into clarity for users and the business.

I started as a front-end developer, moved into product design, and now work as a business analyst and lead designer — translating business needs and requirements into intuitive, end-to-end product flows.

Most of my work is in fintech and complex management platforms: payments, accounting and operations tools used by real businesses every day.
